# **Hollow Deer: N01 Storyboard**

## **Opening: The Theft (\~40 sec)**

Plays after Start on the title screen and loads the tutorial when it ends

| \# | Shot | Visual | Caption / Audio | Sec |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| O1 | Wide | Moonlit forest clearing. The deer sleeps under a tree, its antlers glowing softly. | "Deep in the old forest, a deer's antlers kept the woods alive." Calm ambient music. | 7 |
| O2 | Close-up | The glowing antlers. Light pulses out to nearby plants. | No caption. Soft chime. | 4 |
| O3 | Medium | See the Wolf’s eyes and in the bushes. The wolf is draped in stolen trinkets. | "But one wolf could never resist a treasure that wasn't his." Music cuts out. | 6 |
| O4 | Action | Silhouette of the wolf tearing the antlers away. White screen flash. | Snap SFX, deer cry. | 3 |
| O5 | Wide | The deer, now without antlers, watches the wolf flee into a cave in the hillside. The forest begins to dim. | "He fled into the dungeon below, and the forest began to fade." | 7 |
| O6 | Medium | At the cave mouth, the deer picks up an old scythe left at the entrance. | "The deer would take them back." Fade to black, then load the tutorial. | 6 |

## **Ending: Antler Recovery (\~35 sec)**

Starts when the player picks up the antlers after defeating the Wolf

| \# | Shot | Visual | Caption / Audio | Sec |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| E1 | Medium | The Wolf collapses in the arena and the antlers fall from his grip. | No caption. Boss music fades out. | 4 |
| E2 | Close-up | The antlers settle back onto the deer's head and flare with light. | Swelling chime. | 4 |
| E3 | Medium | Without the antlers' power, the Wolf has shrunk back to a scrawny, ordinary wolf. He slinks off into the dark. | "Without his stolen power, the wolf was just a lonesome wolf again." | 6 |
| E4 | Wide | Light spreads up through the dungeon and the darkness dissolves. | "The antlers' light reached every corner of the dungeon." | 6 |
| E5 | Wide | The forest clearing is restored and glowing, with the deer standing in the center. | "And the forest bloomed once more." Ending theme. | 7 |
| E6 | Title card | The "Hollow Deer" logo. | Fade into the credits screen | 4 |
